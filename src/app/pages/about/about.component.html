Our Story

A Trusted Name in Security & Communication

SwitchIn is a trusted name in the field of tech and network services, delivering reliable communication and surveillance solutions across Odisha. We specialize in installing and supporting advanced security systems, networking towers, and communication devices.

Our mission is to ensure safety and connectivity for every business and home we serve. With a strong commitment to innovation and customer satisfaction, we provide high-quality products and services like CCTV cameras, walkie-talkies, radios, and metal detectors.

Whether it's a small-scale setup or a full enterprise deployment, our team brings years of experience and passion to every project.

Our Mission

To provide top-quality security and communication solutions that protect businesses and connect communities, with an unwavering focus on reliability, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become Eastern India's most trusted technology partner for security infrastructure, wireless communication, and modern IT solutions, empowering businesses of all sizes.
